About Django Blog

Django Blog is a simple yet powerful blogging platform built using the Django framework. It allows users to share ideas, stories, and knowledge in an easy and organized way.

With Django Blog, you can:

  • Create and publish your own posts
  • Edit or delete your content anytime
  • Read blogs written by other users
  • Connect with people who share your interests

This project is created to practice full-stack development using:

  • Python & Django (backend)
  • HTML, CSS, Bootstrap (frontend)
  • SQLite / PostgreSQL (database)

The goal of Django Blog is to learn, share, and grow together in the journey of web development and blogging.
